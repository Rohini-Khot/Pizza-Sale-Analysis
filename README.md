# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis

# 📌 Project Overview
This project provides an in-depth analysis of pizza sales using SQL, uncovering key insights such as total orders, revenue, top-selling pizzas, and category-wise sales. The goal is to optimize inventory, pricing, and marketing strategies based on data-driven findings.

# 🎯 Objectives

Retrieve total orders and revenue

Identify the highest-priced pizza

Determine the most popular pizza sizes and types

Analyze sales distribution by category and time of day

Assess revenue contributions by pizza type and category

Provide actionable recommendations for growth


# 🔍 Key Findings

Total Orders: 21,350

Total Revenue: $817,860.05

Highest-Priced Pizza: Greek Pizza ($35.95)

Most Popular Size: Large (L) – 18,526 orders

# Top 5 Pizzas Ordered:

Classic Deluxe (2,453 orders)

Barbecue Chicken (2,432 orders)

Hawaiian (2,422 orders)

Pepperoni (2,418 orders)

Thai Chicken (2,371 orders)

# Peak Ordering Hours: 12-1 PM & 5-7 PM

# Top Revenue Contributors: Thai Chicken, Barbecue Chicken, California Chicken


# 📈 Recommendations

✅ Inventory & Pricing: Optimize Greek Pizza pricing & bundle deals for bestsellers

✅ Size Optimization: Maintain ample stock for Large (L) pizzas & upsell from Medium (M)

✅ Peak Hour Strategy: Adjust staffing & introduce pre-orders for efficiency

✅ Category Promotions: Leverage targeted deals for Classic (26.91%) & Supreme (25.46%) pizzas

✅ Marketing Focus: Promote top revenue-generating pizzas via social media & meal deals

✅ Increase Daily Orders: Launch lunch specials & expand delivery reach
